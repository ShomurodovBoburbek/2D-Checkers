How to Play Checkers

1. Movement: Pieces move diagonally one square forward on dark tiles.

2. Capturing: Jump over an opponent's piece diagonally into an empty space behind it to capture it.

3. King Promotion: When a piece reaches the furthest row on your opponent's side, it becomes a King (👑). Kings can move and jump both forwards and backwards.

4. Winning: You win by capturing all of the opponent's pieces or trapping them so they have no legal moves left.
